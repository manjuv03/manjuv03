## About Me

I'm an MSc Data Science student at **Queen Mary University of London** (2026 – 2027), focused on **machine learning, deep learning, NLP and large-scale data engineering**.

Before my master's I spent two years as an **Associate Consultant at EY**, validating and optimising ETL pipelines across 50+ interfaces on Databricks and Spark, streaming data with Kafka, and building AI-driven classification models for compliance workflows across FMCG, Retail and Oil & Gas. I've published two research papers, on deep learning for sensitive data classification and on gene expression analysis for leukemia detection, and I'm a **Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate**.
