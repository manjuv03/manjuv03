## About Me

I'm an MSc Data Science student at **Queen Mary University of London** (2026 – 2027), focused on **machine learning, deep learning, NLP and large-scale data engineering**.

Before my master's I spent two years as an **Associate Consultant at EY**, validating and optimising ETL pipelines across 50+ interfaces on Databricks and Spark, streaming data with Kafka, and building AI-driven classification models for compliance workflows across FMCG, Retail and Oil & Gas. Earlier I interned as a **Data Scientist at Amazon**, working on dynamic pricing, and as a **Data Analyst at Village Company**, working on customer retention and churn.

I've published two research papers, on deep learning for sensitive data classification and on gene expression analysis for leukemia detection, and I'm a **Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate**.

> 💼 **Open to:** Data Scientist, Data Engineer, AI Engineer and Machine Learning Engineer roles
>
> 🤝 **Let's collaborate:** I'm always happy to team up on AI, ML and data projects, research or open-source work. Feel free to connect on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/manju-swaroop-v/) or drop me an [Email](mailto:manjuswaroop03@gmail.com).
